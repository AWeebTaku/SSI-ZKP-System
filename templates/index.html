Cross-Border Travel Verification

A self-sovereign identity system where visa credentials are issued off-chain, anchored on an EVM ledger, held by the traveler, and checked at the border using hash-commitment proofs -- no raw personal data ever touches the verifier or the chain.

I

Issuer Console

Issue signed visa credentials, anchor them on-chain, and broadcast revocations.
W

Traveler Wallet

Inspect held credentials, blinding salts, and generate selective-disclosure proofs.
C

Customs Portal

Submit a credential ID and blinded proof to verify status against on-chain state.

Trust flow

  1. Issuer signs traveler data (HMAC-SHA256) and anchors a commitment hash on DidRegistry.sol.
  2. Holder wallet stores the full credential locally, including the blinding salt.
  3. Holder presents only SHA256(status || salt) -- never the salt or PII.
  4. Verifier (Customs) checks the commitment and isRevoked() on-chain. This is a hash-commitment demonstration, not a zk-SNARK.
