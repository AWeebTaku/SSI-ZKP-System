Self-sovereign identity / EVM registry

Cross-border verification without handing over the passport.

A tripartite demo connecting an issuer, a holder wallet, and customs through blinded hash-commitment proofs anchored to an EVM smart contract.

01 / ISSUER

Issue and revoke

Signs a credential, anchors an opaque commitment, and can publish revocation state.

02 / HOLDER

Keep the details

Stores the credential and computes a selective-disclosure proof in the browser.

03 / CUSTOMS

Verify the claim

Receives an ID and proof hash, never the traveler’s raw data or blinding salt.
